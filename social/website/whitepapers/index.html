Adoption framework

The Three Phases of Agentic AI Adoption in Software Engineering

Most teams will soon be able to say they use agents. That alone will not mean they have transformed software delivery. This paper explains the path from Driven to Delegated to Native, and why the hardest crossing is integrating agents into the development lifecycle.

Anatomy of an Agentic Team

Tool Boundaries for Containerized Agents

Containerized agents need tools, but not every tool belongs in every backend image. This paper separates model runtimes, project-local toolchains, and external authority gateways so agent infrastructure can grow without turning every backend into a universal workstation.
